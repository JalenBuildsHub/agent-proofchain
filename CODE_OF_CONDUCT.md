# Code of conduct

Agent ProofChain is a security-sensitive open-source project. Productive participation depends on respectful technical disagreement, careful handling of sensitive information, and accurate claims about what the software does and does not prove.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers should:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- describe uncertainty, limitations, and failed experiments honestly;
- use synthetic examples unless a maintainer explicitly approves another source;
- protect private prompts, credentials, customer data, internal paths, and incident details;
- give actionable review feedback;
- respect maintainers' decisions about project scope and security boundaries;
- correct mistakes promptly and without defensiveness.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not accepted:

- harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, stalking, or doxxing;
- publishing credentials, private data, or identifying incident material;
- irresponsible disclosure of exploitable vulnerabilities;
- deliberately misleading benchmark, security, or adoption claims;
- submitting attack material intended to harm real systems;
- repeated disruption after a maintainer has explained a project boundary;
- impersonation or misrepresentation of another contributor, organization, or provider.

## Security-sensitive discussions

Do not use public issues for undisclosed vulnerabilities or live exploit details. Follow `SECURITY.md` and use the private reporting channel.

Synthetic adversarial examples are welcome when they are clearly labeled, non-operational against real targets, and safe to publish.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, restrict participation, or report conduct to GitHub when necessary to protect contributors, users, systems, or private information.

Reports about community conduct may be sent to `contact@nymrel.com`. Include enough context for review, but do not send unrelated secrets or private customer data.

Maintainers will review reports as privately and fairly as practical. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is not accepted.
