# Open-source program stack

The project remains provider-neutral. Funding, credits, research access, and partnerships
must use separate cost centers and deliverables.

## Recommended sequence

### 1. Public proof

Before applications:

- Complete the portable receipt specification and verifier.
- Publish a 20–50-task synthetic evaluation pilot.
- Tag `v0.1.0` after independent review.
- Publish benchmark methodology, limitations, and machine-readable results.
- Obtain at least two outside reviews, users, or contributors.

### 2. OpenAI Codex Open Source Fund

Primary engineering target. Request credits for the OpenAI adapter, adversarial test
generation, cross-model regression runs, and maintainer automation.

Official application: https://openai.com/form/codex-open-source-fund/

### 3. Anthropic research routes

- External Researcher Access: small Claude safety-research pilot.
- Third-party model evaluations: separate scaled unauthorized-action and receipt-integrity corpus.

Official sources:

- https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/9125743-what-is-the-external-researcher-access-program
- https://www.anthropic.com/news/a-new-initiative-for-developing-third-party-model-evaluations

### 4. Startup and distribution routes

- Anthropic Startup Program only through a verified official, investor, accelerator, or partner route.
- Claude Partner Network after the studio has certified practitioners, production customers,
  and a public customer story.
- OpenAI Codex for Open Source after measurable adoption and recurring maintenance evidence.
- Anthropic Marketplace or equity programs only after an explicit business-model decision.

Official sources:

- https://www.anthropic.com/startup-program-official-terms
- https://claude.com/partners
- https://openai.com/form/codex-for-oss/

## Non-conflict ledger

Track for every program:

- provider and award
- account and owner
- credit expiration
- permitted models and activities
- deliverable and milestone
- model runs and cost center
- publication, publicity, logo, and review obligations
- overlapping support disclosed

Never charge the same model run, engineering milestone, or evaluation task to two programs.
Preregister evaluation scoring and give every provider the same remediation/comment window.

## Acceptance review

Before accepting support, review exclusivity, intellectual property, feedback rights,
publication review, confidentiality, competitor-comparison rules, data retention/training,
taxes, trademarks, publicity, responsible disclosure, and credit expiration.

No application or award may imply that Agent ProofChain is certified, approved, or endorsed
unless the provider grants that language explicitly.
